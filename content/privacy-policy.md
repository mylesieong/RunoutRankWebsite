## 2. Data stored on your device

If your device or platform backup is enabled (for example Android Auto Backup or iCloud device backup), a copy of this local data may be included in your own backup, which is controlled by you and your platform account, not by us.

## 3. Analytics data we collect

We use **Google Firebase Analytics** (a Google service) to measure feature usage, retention, crashes in usage flows, and general app performance so we can decide what to improve. Firebase Analytics collects and processes, on our behalf:

- **App instance identifier** — a random Firebase-generated ID for your installation of the app
- **Advertising identifier** — the Android Advertising ID (AAID) on Android, and, where available, the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) or the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) on iOS
- **Device and technical data** — device model, operating system version, app version, language, country/region derived from IP address, screen and locale settings
- **Usage events** — for example app opens, screens viewed, a test started or completed, a practice level selected, a share action, session length and frequency
- **Approximate location** — coarse region/country inferred from IP address only; the app does **not** request or use precise GPS location

We use the **advertising identifier** at this stage solely to keep our analytics measurement consistent (deduplicating installs and attributing app opens). We do **not** use it to serve targeted advertising, we do **not** build advertising profiles, and we do **not** share it with advertising networks or data brokers. If we later change this, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

We do not attempt to identify you personally from analytics data, and we do not combine analytics data with any other identifying source.

**Legal basis (EEA/UK):** where consent is required for analytics identifiers, we rely on the consent you give when installing and using the app, and you can object to or withdraw it by contacting us as described in Section 7. Otherwise we rely on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app.

We may also record a non-identifying analytics event when a subscription screen is viewed or a purchase completes, so we can measure conversion. This does not include payment details.

## 7. Your choices and controls

**Reset or limit the advertising identifier**

- **Android:** Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → *Reset advertising ID* or *Delete advertising ID*. Deleting it causes the app to receive a string of zeros instead.
- **iOS:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If we ever request tracking permission, you can decline; if you do not grant it, the app does not access the IDFA.

**Analytics**

The app does not currently offer an in-app setting to turn analytics off. You can limit the identifiers involved by resetting or deleting your advertising ID as described above, and collection stops entirely if you uninstall the app. You may also object to our analytics processing, or ask us to delete analytics data associated with your installation, by contacting us at the address in Section 10.

**Delete your data**

Because your test and practice data lives only on your device, deleting it is entirely in your control: use the reset option in the app's settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. To request deletion of analytics data associated with your app instance, contact us at the address in Section 10 — the request is easiest to fulfil if you have not yet reset your advertising ID.

**Your rights**

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, or similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Contact us at the address in Section 10 and we will respond within the period required by applicable law. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
